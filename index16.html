About Me

Hello, I'm Marie! I’ve been practicing numerology for 14 years, and my love for numbers, patterns, and the hidden connections in our lives has only continued to grow. I’ve always been drawn to the way numbers can offer new perspectives on who we are, the paths we take, and the cycles we experience. Alongside numerology, I love tarot, astrology and nature.

The name Gardenumerology brings together two things I love: the natural world and the fascinating world of numerology. To me, both are full of patterns, cycles, and opportunities for growth. Just as a garden changes with the seasons, we move through different chapters in life, each offering something new to discover. I created Gardenumerology as a space to explore these connections, share insights, and inspire others to learn more about themselves through the language of numbers.

Sharing my passion for numerology and these topics brings me so much joy, and I’m grateful to have a place where I can connect with others who share these interests. If you’ve ever liked, commented on, or interacted with any of my posts, thank you! Your support means more than you know, and I’m happy you’re here. I hope Gardenumerology inspires your curiosity, encourages self-discovery, and helps you find a little more meaning in the patterns of your own life.
